# Marketing-site photography — the slot plan (2026-07-27)

Eric asked for photos on the marketing site; sourcing is **Visual Studio
(AI-generated)** per his pick, with existing repo photos recommended where a
real photo already exists. A 3-auditor sweep mapped every page; 15 slots
earned a photo, 27 sections were deliberately skipped (forms, dark closers,
wizard tools, and anywhere imagery would fabricate evidence — fake clients,
mocked search results, stand-in portfolio work).

**The honesty law for this pass (matches the overhaul's copy law):** no
AI-generated humans presented as real people or clients — generated imagery
is spaces, tools, food, textures, and hands-at-work detail crops only, with
claim-free captions. Real people on this site = the real founder only.

## Implementation notes (for the build pass, once images arrive)

- Files land at repo root as `photo-<slot>.jpg` (auto-ships via
  build-public.sh's `cp ./*.jpg`); index strip tiles as `photo-strip-*.jpg`.
- Every `<img>` gets width/height attributes (no CLS), `loading="lazy"`,
  `decoding="async"`, honest alt text; figures reuse `.hww-shot` /
  life-strip treatments (16px radius, soft shadow, mono figcaption).
- Captions stay claim-free on generated images (e.g. "Every site starts on
  paper." — not "my desk"). No generated image may imply it depicts the
  founder, a client, client work, or results.
- Dark scheme: figures keep their light imagery (photograph-as-artifact,
  same as the pmock precedent); frames/captions follow the page's dark
  counterparts. Contrast of captions measured ≥4.5:1 both schemes.
- Red-first pins: presence + anti-CLS attributes + alt text + the
  no-fake-humans rule can't be pinned mechanically, but file presence,
  dimensions, lazy/decoding attrs, and caption contrast can and will be.
- Optional Eric call: add one line to /ai-disclaimer noting decorative
  imagery may be AI-generated (honest-forward; not legally required).
