# Cookie Policy

**Studio OS Presence.** Effective **[[OWNER: date]]**.

## What we use
Studio OS Presence uses **only essential storage** to keep you signed in and to run the app. Specifically:

- **Authentication/session** — the app stores your session token in your browser's local storage (key `dds-portal-auth`) so you stay signed in. This is strictly necessary for the service to function.

We do **not** use:
- third-party advertising cookies,
- cross-site tracking or fingerprinting,
- analytics cookies that identify you individually.

Because we use only strictly-necessary storage, **no cookie-consent banner is required** for the application. If we later add non-essential (e.g. analytics) cookies, we will add a consent banner and update this policy first.

## Managing storage
You can clear this storage in your browser at any time; doing so signs you out. Blocking essential storage will prevent the app from keeping you signed in.
