Between **[[OWNER: legal entity]]** ("Processor," "we") and the customer ("Controller," "you"). ## 1. Roles
For the content you place in your workspace and any personal data it contains (e.g. contacts you publish), **you are the controller and we are the processor**. For your own account/billing data, we act as an independent controller (see the [Privacy Policy](PRIVACY-POLICY.md)).

## 2. Scope & instructions
We process personal data only to provide the service and on your documented instructions (your use of the product, these terms, and your configuration). We do not sell personal data or use it for our own advertising.

## 3. Subject matter, duration, nature, purpose
- **Subject matter/purpose:** hosting, rendering, publishing, backing up, and — where enabled — AI-assisting and connected-data reading for your online presence.
- **Duration:** for the life of your account plus the retention windows in the [Data Retention Policy](DATA-RETENTION-POLICY.md).
- **Categories of data subjects:** you, your team, and individuals whose details you publish.
- **Categories of data:** business contact details, content, media, and (if enabled) connected-service read data. No special-category data is required or intended.

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures: deny-all row-level security with per-workspace isolation; encryption in transit; AES-256-GCM encryption of connected-service tokens stored out-of-row; approval-gated external changes; and an append-only audit log that records field names, not values. Full description: [Security Statement](SECURITY-STATEMENT.md). **[[OWNER: a formal third-party penetration test is recommended and pending — note its status.]]**

## 6. Confidentiality

## 7. Data-subject rights & assistance
We assist you in responding to data-subject requests via the product's export, correction, deletion, and disconnect features, and by reasonable support for account-level erasure ([Account Deletion Policy](ACCOUNT-DELETION-POLICY.md)).

## 8. Personal-data breach
