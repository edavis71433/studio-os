## Who we are
We are the **data controller** for your account information and the **data processor** for the content you place in your workspace (see the [DPA](DPA.md)). We keep data practices deliberately minimal.

## Data we collect

**Account & signup:** your email and business name; a single-use email-verification token. We do **not** record your IP address or device/user-agent at signup.

**Business content (mostly public by design):** the facts you enter to publish your site — business name, phone, email, address, offerings, FAQs, posts, testimonials — and your uploaded photos, brand profile, and knowledge documents. Most of this is intended to appear publicly on your website.

**Billing:** your email, business name, chosen plan, and subscription status. **We do not store your payment-card details** — those are handled by Stripe (see [Subprocessors](SUBPROCESSORS.md)).

**Connected services (only if you connect them):** an encrypted access token and the plain business numbers we read (e.g. review count) — read-only by default.

**Operational:** a change/audit log that records **which fields changed, never their values**; usage counts for AI (never shown to you as a number, never sold).

We do **not** collect special-category data (health, biometrics, government IDs) or track you across the web.

## How we use it
To operate your workspace: render and publish your site, run the calm daily intelligence, process billing, and — only when you ask — assist with drafting and connected insights. We do not sell your data or use it for third-party advertising.

## AI
When you use an AI feature, we send only what's needed and nothing more: the **business facts** you're drafting from (the Writer) or a **text description** you provide (the Visual Studio). We do **not** send your account credentials, your connected tokens, or **your uploaded photos** to AI models. The Concierge answers from your own data and does not call an AI model. You review and approve every AI output; what you approve is yours. See the [AI Policy](AI-POLICY.md).

## Connected services
Access is read-only unless you approve a specific change; tokens are encrypted and never shown to anyone; **disconnecting destroys our access and deletes the cached data**. See the [Connected Platform Terms](CONNECTED-PLATFORM-TERMS.md).

## Sharing
We share data only with the subprocessors that run the service (hosting, payments, email, and — when you enable them — AI and connected providers). Each is listed in the [Subprocessor List](SUBPROCESSORS.md). We disclose data if legally required, and would notify you where lawful.

## Storage, security, retention
Data is hosted with Supabase; sensitive tokens are encrypted (AES-256-GCM) and stored separately; access is deny-all by default and scoped to your own workspace. See the [Security Statement](SECURITY-STATEMENT.md). We keep data until you delete it; published versions and audit logs are kept for the ownership and traceability guarantees; connected data is cleared on disconnect. Full detail: [Data Retention Policy](DATA-RETENTION-POLICY.md).
