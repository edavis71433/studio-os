# Account Deletion Policy

You can remove your data from Studio OS Presence at the level you need. This policy states honestly what is self-serve today and what is handled on request.

## Self-serve (do it yourself, immediately)
- **Delete media:** remove any photo (the image file is deleted from storage; refused only while it's in use, which names what's using it).
- **Delete content:** remove offerings, FAQs, posts, and other content from your workspace and site.
- **Disconnect a service:** disconnecting a connected provider **immediately destroys our stored access token and deletes the cached data** — after that we cannot access your account there.
- **Export first:** you can [export everything](DATA-EXPORT-POLICY.md) before deleting anything.

## Full-account deletion (on request)
A single self-serve "delete my entire account" button is **not yet available**; full-account erasure is performed on request:
1. Request deletion from **[[OWNER: privacy@… / in-app request]]**.
2. We verify you own the account.
3. We delete your workspace content, media, brand, knowledge, connected tokens/data, and account record within **[[OWNER: e.g. 30]]** days.
4. **Exceptions:** we retain what the law requires (e.g. billing/tax records) and copies that exist in rolling backups until the backup window expires.
5. We confirm completion.

*(A self-serve account-deletion endpoint is a planned enhancement; see the Privacy Audit risk register R1. This policy is accurate to current behavior.)*

## After deletion
Deletion is permanent and not recoverable through the app. Export beforehand if you want a copy.
