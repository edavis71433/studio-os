# Customer Ownership Guarantee

*This guarantee reflects binding Product Laws in the [Constitution](../presence/constitution/) and is enforced by the software.*

**Your content is yours.** Your business facts, text, photos, brand, and knowledge documents belong to you. We take only the limited license needed to host, render, publish, and back up your site.

**AI-assisted work is yours.** Drafts and images you approve are yours — the same as anything you type. We record that they were AI-assisted (`ai_approved`) for honesty only, never to limit your use. AI never publishes on its own, and every AI feature has a manual equivalent that is never slower or hidden.

**Your domain is yours.** Connecting or pointing a domain never transfers it. You keep it.

**No lock-in.** You can [export](DATA-EXPORT-POLICY.md) everything — your content, media references, brand, redirects, and a fully rendered, framework-free copy of your website — at any time, and host it anywhere. Nothing in the export requires Studio OS.

**No metered fees, ever.** We bill the site, not the seat, and never charge overage or per-action fees (Product Law 20).

**Your connected accounts stay yours.** We read only what you authorize, only in read-only mode unless you approve a specific change, and disconnecting destroys our access — your account and its data are untouched.

**You can leave.** Cancel any time; export your data; even after cancellation you retain read-only access and export for a grace period ([Subscription & Billing](SUBSCRIPTION-AND-BILLING.md), [Data Retention](DATA-RETENTION-POLICY.md)).

These are not marketing promises — they are enforced by the architecture (one renderer, versioned publishing, the export endpoint, encrypted revocable connections, and the approval spine) and verified in the [Data Governance & Privacy Audit](../presence/DATA-GOVERNANCE-PRIVACY-AUDIT.md).
