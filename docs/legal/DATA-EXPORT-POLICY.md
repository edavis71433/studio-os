# Data Export Policy

Studio OS Presence gives you a complete, portable copy of everything you own — at any time, with no barrier. This reflects the export capability in the software (`GET /export`).

## What you get
A single export bundle (`studio-os-export/1`) containing:
- **Your structured content** — business facts, offerings, FAQs, posts, testimonials, settings.
- **Your media references** — image list with alt text and dimensions (download the image files from your library).
- **Your brand profile** and **knowledge documents**.
- **Your redirects.**
- **A fully rendered, framework-free copy of your website** (HTML/CSS) — hostable anywhere, requiring nothing from Studio OS.

The bundle includes an ownership note stating plainly that everything in it is yours.

## How & when
- Available on demand from your workspace at any time, including during a grace period after cancellation.
- No fee, no waiting period, no "contact sales" gate.

## Format & portability
Open, documented JSON plus standard HTML/CSS — designed so you can leave and host your presence elsewhere without us.
