# Accessibility Statement

We want Studio OS Presence — and the websites it produces — to work for everyone, including people who use assistive technology.

## Our commitment
We design toward **WCAG 2.1 AA** as our target standard. **[[OWNER: confirm the target level and any legal obligation (ADA/EN 301 549) for your market.]]**

## What's in place today (verified)
The customer application is built with accessibility foundations:
- **Keyboard operable** controls with a visible focus indicator (`:focus-visible`).
- **Semantic structure and ARIA** — labels on interactive and status elements, live-region status messages.
- **Reduced-motion** support (animations respect `prefers-reduced-motion`).
- **Readable by default** — light/dark themes, responsive layouts for phone/tablet/desktop, scalable text.
- Published sites are rendered through one accessible template system, and the product requires **alt text on every image** at upload.

## In progress (stated honestly)
A **formal, independent accessibility audit** (screen-reader passes, contrast-ratio and zoom verification across all flows) is **recommended and pending** before public launch. Until it is completed and any findings addressed, we describe our status as "accessibility foundations in place; formal conformance audit in progress."
