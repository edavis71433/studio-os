# L1.5 — Commercial Audit & Pricing Validation

**Documentation only.** No code, no deploy, no pricing change. This validates the commercial model shipped in L1 against the Commercial Constitution (`02-commercial-constitution.md`) and the intelligence architecture, and recommends changes to make **before** the go-live switch is thrown.

Method: read the shipped catalog (`commerce/catalog.ts`), the enforcement points (`middleware/entitlement.ts`, `index.ts`), the provisioning + billing wiring (`commerce/*`), the AI personas (`writer/`, `coach/`, `reviewer/`, `guardian/`, `concierge/`), and the existing telemetry (`ai_calls`, migration 0013). Findings are grounded in what the code actually does, not what the docs intend.

---

## The one structural finding that frames everything

**`plan` is recorded but not enforced.** The entitlement gate (`middleware/entitlement.ts`) reads only `status` (active/paused/lapsed). The only plan/edition-aware behavior in the whole platform is the Monitor publish/restore block (`index.ts:115`). The five-rung `plan` column L1 added drives **nothing** yet — same AI, same personas, same limits for every paid rung.

That is fine as a *launch-day* posture for Monitor vs. Presence (the differentiator there is hosting + publishing, which *is* enforced by edition). It is **not** fine as the answer to "AI usage needs to be included in the plan if they use too much" — there is currently no per-plan allowance, no per-customer accounting, and no upgrade nudge. The AI strategy below is the plan to close that, and it is the single most important pre-launch commercial gap.

---

## Verdicts on the nine verification points

### 1. Every feature belongs to the correct edition — ⚠️ mostly, with one real gap
- **Correct:** hosting, publishing, custom domain, restore, the renderer — all Presence-and-up, enforced by `edition` (Monitor is blocked from publish/restore). Migration readiness and observation — Monitor-and-up. Portfolio/queues/white-label — Agency (gated by the separate agency principal, not the entitlement). This all matches the constitution's feature matrix.
- **Gap:** the AI intelligence stack (Concierge, Growth Coach, Brand Guardian, Reviewer, Draft Writer/Editor) is reachable by **any** active entitlement regardless of plan or edition. A **Monitor** site — which cannot publish — can still run the Draft Writer and Editor whose only purpose is to draft content *for publishing*. That is a feature in the wrong edition. **Recommendation:** scope generative *drafting* (Writer/Editor) to Presence-and-up; keep *observational* AI (Concierge answers, Coach guidance, Brand/Reviewer findings) available to Monitor, because guidance-on-what-you-already-have is exactly Monitor's promise.

### 2. AI usage aligns with the intended plans — ✗ not yet (the core pre-launch item)
- The constitution (Part 5) is explicit: *"the client never sees a credit,"* Assistive AI is unlimited within fair use, Generative AI is *"included with generous soft caps per plan; sustained heavy use surfaces as a plan conversation, never a mid-task paywall,"* backed by *"internal metering events."*
- **Today none of that exists.** The Presence personas call Anthropic (`claude-haiku-4-5`, cheap per call) but **do not log to `ai_calls`**, and `ai_calls` is `tenant_id`-scoped, so even if they did, usage could not be attributed to a customer or compared to a plan allowance.
- This is precisely the user's addition, and it is correct strategy. Formalized in **"The AI Usage Commercial Strategy"** below. **Recommendation:** implement per-plan soft allowances + per-customer accounting + a friendly "you've outgrown this plan" Moment before opening self-serve to strangers, because unlimited generative AI on a $15 Monitor plan is the biggest uncapped cost on the board.

### 3. Presence Monitor value without cannibalizing Presence — ✓ with a guardrail
- The value gap is genuine and defensible: **Monitor watches a site you already have; Presence *is* your site.** Monitor has no hosting, no publishing, no website — the moment a Monitor customer wants a change *made* or *published*, they must upgrade. That is a clean, honest fence.
- **Cannibalization risk** is not the hosting — it's the *AI*. A Presence-appropriate customer could sit on Monitor, harvest Coach/Concierge/Brand guidance, and hand-apply it to their existing site, paying $15 instead of $39. **Recommendation (ties to #1/#2):** keep Monitor's AI *observational and read-only* (guidance, findings, answers), route every "make this change / publish this" affordance to an upgrade, and set Monitor's generative allowance lower than Presence's. Then Monitor sells the *watchfulness*, and *acting* is what you pay Presence for.

### 4. Founder pricing is sustainable — ⚠️ sustainable only once AI is capped
- Shipped founder / list: **Monitor $15/$19 · Presence $39/$49 · Managed $119/$149** (monthly).
- Per-unit costs are low: hosting (Netlify, effectively free/cheap per small site), `claude-haiku` (cents per call), Supabase (shared). The founder rate lock is a *rate* lock, not a *feature* fork (correct per §3.3), so it doesn't compound cost over time.
- **The one real exposure is uncapped generative AI**, concentrated at the cheap end: a $15 Monitor customer running the Coach and Concierge daily could plausibly cost more in model calls than they pay. Founder pricing is sustainable **iff** the AI allowance work (#2) lands first. **Recommendation:** treat "AI allowances shipped" as a launch gate for the $15 tier specifically. The $39 and $119 rungs have comfortable margin even before caps.

### 5. Annual pricing is correct — ✓
- Annual charges 10 months (`ANNUAL_MONTHS_CHARGED = 10`), i.e. **two months free, ~16.7% off**. Math verified: Presence annual = $390 founder / $490 list; Managed = $1,190 / $1,490. Standard, defensible, and it improves cash flow and reduces churn — the "calm of not thinking about it" the constitution names (§3.2). No change needed. **Minor note:** confirm the display copy says "billed yearly" and shows the effective monthly, so the discount is legible (the pricing page already does).

### 6. Upgrade and downgrade paths make sense — ⚠️ logic is right, execution is incomplete
- The *rules* are correct and tested: upgrades instant + prorated, downgrades at renewal with no data loss, changes to/from contact tiers blocked (`evaluateChange`). This matches §3.6–3.7.
- **Two execution gaps:**
  - **No self-serve "change plan" action exists.** `evaluateChange` is pure logic with no route that performs the change. Today a customer can *cancel* (Billing Portal) but not *upgrade/downgrade* in-product unless Stripe's Billing Portal is configured with switchable products — which is a dashboard step, not code.
  - **A Monitor→Presence upgrade would not provision hosting.** `billing-sync` on `customer.subscription.updated` calls `applyEntitlementPatch` (updates the `plan`) but only calls `provisionForSignup` when the entitlement row is *missing*. So an existing Monitor customer who upgrades would get `plan = presence` **while their site stays `edition = monitor` with no hosting** — a broken upgrade. **Recommendation (pre-launch fix, L2):** when a lifecycle event changes the plan such that edition changes, re-run provisioning (flip edition + provision hosting). Until then, upgrades that cross the Monitor→Presence boundary must be operator-assisted.

### 7. Agency pricing scales appropriately — ✓ as *deferred*, not as *built*
- Correctly marked **not self-serve** (`selfServe: false`, no online price); the catalog routes Agency to "contact us," honoring §3.10 (wholesale per-active-site brackets or referral mode).
- **But the billing mechanics don't exist:** `presence_agencies.plan` is a free-text label; there is no per-active-site metering, no volume bracket, no wholesale invoice path. That is *acceptable* — Agency is a later launch rung (§10: "agency beta third") and the entitlement machinery can express it when needed — but it should be stated plainly so no one assumes Agency is commercially ready. **Recommendation:** leave as-is for launch; when Agency opens, bill per active entitlement in the agency's portfolio (the data already exists in `presence_agency_clients`).

### 8. Enterprise assumptions remain valid — ✓
- Correctly **never self-serve** (§3.11), annual contract, governance-over-pipeline. No code path claims otherwise; nothing to build for launch. Assumptions hold. The thin account interface and provenance-as-audit are the seams Enterprise attaches to later. No change.

### 9. Billing and entitlements match the constitutional strategy — ✓ with the caveats above
- **Matches:** bill the site not the seat (§3.1 — entitlement is per client+product, one site); the lifecycle ladder rides the existing three states with a 14-day past-due grace (§3.5) — no new status invented; founder as a rate lock (§3.3); ownership/export untouched (§3.12, Part 4). The webhook stays the single payment truth and delegates to one idempotent provisioner — no parallel money source.
- **Caveats:** the plan dimension is recorded but unenforced (#1), AI allowances are unspecified (#2), and the upgrade execution is incomplete (#6). None of these contradict the constitution; they are unfinished corners of it.

## The AI Usage Commercial Strategy

Formalizing the user's addition, which aligns exactly with constitution Part 5. This is the recommended commercial policy and the shape of its (future) implementation. **No code is written here.**

### Policy (what the customer experiences)
1. **Every paid plan includes generous AI, sized to its audience.** A restaurant owner editing their own hours will never approach the cap; the number exists only to catch true outliers.
2. **The platform meters usage internally.** The customer sees no credits, no counters, no "3 of 10 left" — that anxiety would destroy the value of the assistive tools (Part 5, "the client never sees a credit").
3. **Assistive AI stays effectively unlimited** (alt-text, rewording in voice, FAQ shaping) — cost is trivial and fear-of-using-it is the real cost.
4. **Generative AI (draft-this-update, seasonal proposals) has a soft monthly allowance per plan.** Exceeding it never blocks mid-task and never charges a surprise fee.
5. **Sustained over-use becomes a warm conversation, not a paywall.** When a customer consistently exceeds their plan's normal envelope, they receive a friendly message — *"You're getting a lot of value from the writing help — you've outgrown this plan. Here's how to add capacity."* — delivered as a **Moment** in the room, not an interstitial. Upgrading raises the allowance.
6. **No plan is ever throttled silently.** A failed/withheld generation is silent-safe (a suggestion that simply doesn't appear), never a degraded experience the customer can't explain.

### Suggested allowances (starting points, to confirm — not prices)
Generative operations per month (drafts, rewrites-as-new, proposals). Assistive is uncapped.

| Plan | Generative envelope (soft) | Rationale |
|---|---|---|
| Presence Monitor | small (e.g. ~15/mo), **observational only** | Monitor guides; it doesn't draft-to-publish. Protects the $15 tier. |
| Presence | generous (e.g. ~60/mo) | A self-run site owner writing updates comfortably never notices. |
| Presence Managed | very generous / effectively unlimited | Concierge does the work; usage is our labor, not their metric. |
| Agency / Enterprise | pooled per contract | Set at signing; governance over allocation, not a per-seat meter. |

These numbers are deliberately illustrative — set them from real telemetry once personas log usage (below).

### Implementation shape (future work, not this milestone)
- **Wire the personas into telemetry.** The `ai_calls` table + `ai_log()` function already exist (migration 0013) but the Presence personas don't call them, and the table is `tenant_id`-scoped. Add `client_id`/`site_id` attribution (additive column) and have each generative call log one row. This is the metering substrate the constitution already reserved (§4.3).
- **Define allowances as data** in the catalog (a field per plan), exactly like prices — one place to tune.
- **A monthly rollup** (a cheap query over `ai_calls` by client) yields "generative ops this cycle." Compare to the plan's envelope.
- **The nudge is a Moment.** When usage exceeds the envelope for N consecutive cycles (not a single spike — seasonal businesses spike legitimately), emit a friendly upgrade Moment through the existing Moments pipeline. It suppresses like any other Moment; it never blocks.
- **Never a hard gate at the SMB level.** Over-cap does not stop generation; it starts a conversation. (Hard ceilings belong only to abuse protection, which is a separate, invisible safeguard.)

This keeps the experience premium (no credits, no meters, no paywalls) while protecting operating cost — precisely the balance the user asked for, and the one the constitution already committed to.

## Pre-launch recommendations, prioritized

**Must-fix before self-serve opens to strangers:**
1. **Ship AI usage allowances + internal metering + the upgrade Moment (#2).** Without it, the $15 tier is an uncapped cost. This is the headline item.
2. **Fix the Monitor→Presence upgrade so it provisions hosting (#6).** A broken upgrade is worse than no self-serve upgrade — until fixed, gate cross-edition upgrades to operator-assisted.
3. **Scope generative drafting to Presence-and-up (#1).** Keep observational AI on Monitor.

**Should-do before or shortly after launch:**
4. **Add a self-serve "change plan" route** (or configure Stripe Billing Portal product-switching) so upgrade/downgrade is real, not just modeled (#6).
5. **Confirm the founder/list prices** (§4) — they are my proposed defaults; the constitution deliberately left numbers to launch.
6. **Document the rung↔constitution mapping** so "Monitor/Presence/Managed" (customer-facing catalog) and "Core/Add-on/Standalone" (constitutional product lines) don't drift — the same reconciliation flagged in M14.5.

**Explicitly leave alone for launch:**
7. Agency billing mechanics (#7) — deferred rung, build when it opens.
8. Enterprise (#8) — assumptions valid, nothing to build.
9. Annual math (#5) — correct as shipped.

## Summary

The L1 commercial model is **structurally sound and constitutionally faithful**: it bills the site not the seat, rides the lifecycle ladder on the existing states, treats founders as a rate lock, and keeps one payment truth and one provisioner. The **naming, ownership, and trust guarantees are correct**.

The gaps are all in the **same unfinished corner**: the `plan` dimension is recorded but not yet *enforced* — which shows up as (a) no AI usage envelope, (b) an incomplete cross-edition upgrade, and (c) generative drafting reachable from an edition that can't publish. The **AI Usage Commercial Strategy** above is both the customer-experience policy and the technical fix for the largest cost exposure ($15 Monitor with unlimited AI), and it aligns exactly with what the constitution already committed to and what the user asked for.

**Nothing here blocks a concierge launch today.** These are the items to close before the doors open to strangers at self-serve prices.

**L1.5 Commercial Validation complete.**
