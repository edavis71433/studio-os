# Presence Intelligence Constitution v1

**Status: RATIFIED & FROZEN — 2026-07-06, in full.** §11's commercial placement was ratified the same day as **Amendment 2** (`06-amendment-2-presence-monitor-edition.md`): Presence Monitor is the entry edition of the one Presence line.
Sits beside: `00`–`04` (all frozen). Nothing here modifies the render contract, the publish pipeline, the API, the schema, or any frozen law. This document is philosophy and boundaries only; it binds every later milestone (M9, M10, and beyond). No implementation exists yet, by design.

---

## 1 · Purpose

Studio OS Presence is not building "AI features." It is building a **Presence Intelligence System** whose entire purpose is:

> Help businesses become easier to **discover**, easier to **trust**, easier to **choose**, and easier to **manage**.

The customer should never feel they are using AI. They should feel that an experienced team — a designer, a developer, an SEO specialist, an accessibility expert, a content strategist, a local marketing advisor, a technical webmaster — is quietly doing its work, without the business owner having to become any of those people.

**The invisibility test (constitutional):** if a feature makes the customer aware they are operating an AI, the feature is wrong — either in framing, in vocabulary, or in existence. The word "AI" itself is not customer-facing vocabulary; the five reserved verbs (03 §3) and the concierge's voice are.

---

## 2 · The Two Systems

Two completely separate subsystems, permanently:

### System One — Presence Intelligence *(it THINKS)*
Explains. Recommends. Writes. Edits. Reviews. Teaches. Guides.
**It never changes reality without approval.** Its only write path into a business's content is the Draft Writer — the same gate everything uses — and only at the rungs of the authority ladder the client has climbed (03 §3). It never touches a live site, a live profile, or a live anything, except through the publish ritual a human (or a human's explicit, revocable standing consent) invokes.

### System Two — Presence Optimization Engine *(it OBSERVES)*
Continuously monitors everything measurable: technical SEO, AEO, accessibility, performance, security headers, local presence, trust signals, business-information consistency, reviews and reputation, content freshness, broken links, media weight and alt coverage, schema/structured data, robots, sitemaps, canonicals, Open Graph, indexing, metadata, website health, business-profile health, conversion opportunities.
**It produces evidence. It never proposes, never writes, never judges.** It has no write path at all — not even the Draft Writer. Its output is a growing body of dated, sourced observations.

### The separation rules (permanent)
1. **Evidence flows one way:** Optimization Engine → Presence Intelligence. Intelligence consumes evidence; it never manufactures it. The Engine never consumes Intelligence output.
2. **Only Intelligence speaks to the customer,** and only through the concierge grammar (moments, recommendations, drafts). Raw engine output is operator/internal material.
3. **Neither system publishes.** Publication belongs to the pipeline, invoked by humans through the ritual. This line is absolute.
4. The full causal chain is always: **observation → evidence → judgment → proposal → human decision → Draft Writer → draft → ritual → live.** No arrow may be skipped, reversed, or automated past the rung the client chose.

---

## 3 · Binding to the Five Verbs

Everything either system surfaces must wear exactly one of the five reserved words (03 §3, permanent): **Assist** (in-field, invited), **Suggest** (a note; writes nothing), **Draft** (writes to the draft, on request), **Prepare** (sets work aside for review), **Auto-publish** (rung 4; explicit, revocable standing consent). A capability that fits none of the five words does not ship. This constitution adds no sixth word.

## 4 · Personas — the invisible team

Personas are **internal divisions of labor, not customer-facing characters**. The customer meets exactly one voice: the concierge. Behind that voice:

| Persona | Mandate (and only this) | Speaks via |
|---|---|---|
| **Writer** | Produces new prose from client-provided facts: descriptions, answers, updates, metadata, alt text. Never invents a fact; where a fact is missing, it produces a question instead. | Draft / Prepare |
| **Editor** | Improves existing prose: rewrite, expand, shorten, simplify, clarify, tone — always within the voice profile. Touches only what it was handed. | Assist / Draft |
| **Reviewer** | Judges content against readability, consistency, completeness, and truth-to-facts. Produces findings, never fixes. | Suggest |
| **Strategist** | Turns evidence and calendar into priorities: what deserves attention this week and why. Owns sequencing, never content. | Suggest (moments) |
| **Brand Guardian** | Enforces the voice profile and the never-claim list; blocks any output that drifts from the business's voice or asserts what the client didn't provide. A veto, not a author. | (internal gate) |
| **Accessibility Reviewer** | Audits content-level accessibility (alt coverage, heading sense, link text, readability). Template-level accessibility belongs to the template bar (03 §4), not to this persona. | Suggest |
| **SEO Reviewer** | Judges discoverability evidence: metadata, structure, internal links, schema completeness, AEO answerability. | Suggest |
| **Local Presence Advisor** | Judges consistency and completeness of the business's local footprint (hours, address, profile parity) against evidence. | Suggest |
| **Growth Coach** | Spots opportunities in the business's own rhythm: seasons, holidays, quiet sections, unused photographs. Encourages, never nags (dedupe law applies). | Suggest (moments) |
| **Business Consultant** | The long view: reads outcomes memory (what the client accepted, dismissed, published) and adjusts what the team raises. The reason the product gets quieter and more right over time. | (internal; shapes others) |
| **Concierge** | The host. The single voice that carries every moment, recommendation, and question to the customer, in the house grammar. Owns tone, timing, and restraint. | All customer-facing surfaces |

**No overlap rule:** a persona may not perform another's mandate. **One host rule:** no persona ever addresses the customer directly; everything is spoken by the concierge, in one voice. The customer never sees the team — only its care.

## 5 · Business Moments

The primary customer experience is not a dashboard and never will be. It is a **Business Moment**: one calm, aware sentence-or-two about the business, at most a handful per day, spoken by the concierge on Today.

**The moment grammar** (extends the frozen note grammar): a moment has a *statement* (serif, human), at most one *reason*, at most one *action*, and *dismiss-means-gone*. Kinds remain caution > suggestion > celebration. Sources: internal health models, Optimization Engine evidence, the business calendar (holidays, seasons, anniversaries), the client's own rhythm (publish cadence, section freshness), and — later — destinations (reviews, search patterns).

Canonical examples (the register to hold):
- *Good morning. Everything customers can see is current.*
- *One thing deserves your attention — Saturday's hours disagree between your site and your Google profile.*
- *Labor Day is two weeks out. Want to say whether you're open?*
- *Customers searching for "patio dining near Echo Park" are finding you. Your patio has no photograph.*
- *Your reviews improved this week — two new kind words worth keeping.*
- *Your menu hasn't changed in six months. Still true?*

**Restraint laws:** at most three active moments (frozen M7 cap holds); a dismissed moment never returns for the same facts (dedupe holds); celebrations must be earned, never manufactured; a day with nothing worth saying says *everything is okay* and stops. Awareness, not surveillance: moments come from the business's own presence and public signals, never from tracking its visitors (Law 22).

## 6 · The Recommendation Contract

Every recommendation — every moment with an action, every Prepared item, every review finding — carries five clauses, always:

1. **Why** — the reason, in merchant words.
2. **Evidence** — the dated observation(s) behind it. *No recommendation without evidence* (new Law 31). "Best practice" is not evidence.
3. **Expected benefit** — what should get better, honestly hedged ("customers checking hours will see the right ones" — never invented numbers).
4. **Estimated effort** — in the customer's time ("about a minute," "ten minutes with photos in hand").
5. **Undoability** — what is recoverable and how ("fully undoable — your draft is set aside as a safety copy"; "publishing replaces the live page; every version is kept").

The customer should never wonder *why is it telling me this* or *what happens if I say yes*.

## 7 · Writing capabilities

Everything Presence Intelligence can ever write, in four classes. All writing lands in **structured content through the Draft Writer** — never in HTML, never past the contract, never bypassing Snapshot → Renderer → FileMap → Pipeline.

**Class A — within today's content contract (M9 era):** business description, story, taglines; service/offering names and descriptions; FAQs and answers; updates/announcements (the contract's posts — "blog" is not our word); seasonal update copy; testimonials handling (formatting, never authoring — kind words are only ever quoted); CTAs within template regions; metadata (SEO titles/descriptions); alt text; structured-data *suggestions* (schema fills from facts — emitted by the renderer, suggested by Intelligence).

**Class B — projections to destinations (M10+):** Google Business Profile posts, social posts, customer emails/newsletters — each through its destination's projection contract, each derived from structured content, each reviewed before it leaves.

**Class C — contract evolution (future, additive or major per the frozen versioning rules):** landing pages, seasonal pages, policy pages, new page kinds. Intelligence may *propose* a new page; the page kind must exist in a template's manifest before anything renders. Page generation never precedes contract support.

**Class D — whole-presence generation (the horizon):** complete starter sites and industry-specific starter content — an intake conversation producing a *fully drafted, never published* structured-content draft on a Studio template. Generation is drafting at scale; the ritual still stands between the draft and the world.

**The fact law, mechanically (Law 11 sharpened):** every generated claim must trace to a client-provided fact, an existing published fact, or engine evidence. Where a fact is missing, the Writer's output is a *question to the client*, never an invention. The Brand Guardian vetoes unattributable claims before anything is shown.

## 8 · Review capabilities

Reviews judge; they never fix. Every review consumes Optimization Engine evidence and/or the draft itself, and produces findings in the Recommendation Contract. The review surface covers: SEO, AEO answerability, accessibility (content level), brand/voice adherence, business-information consistency (site vs profiles), trust signals, readability, conversion (weak or missing CTAs), freshness, internal links, broken links, missing content (empty sections a customer would expect), media (coverage, weight, alt), schema completeness. A review that finds nothing says so and disappears — findings must earn their existence (Law 23).

---

## 9 · Editing capabilities

Rewrite · expand · shorten · simplify · improve tone · improve clarity · translate · localize — always inside the field or entity the client is working with (Assist) or on explicit request (Draft), always within the voice profile (tone notes, preferred vocabulary, never-claim list — the brand contract that already exists in the schema). Every edit shows old → new and reverts in one tap. **Translation/localization note:** editing prose is available anytime; *localized sites* remain a contract-major concern per the frozen roadmap — the Editor may translate a description long before the platform renders a bilingual site.

## 10 · Presence Optimization Engine — the observation plane

The Engine watches everything §2 lists, continuously and calmly. Constitutional properties:

- **Evidence record:** every observation is dated, sourced (which check, which URL, which fetch), typed, and kept — evidence has history, so "improved this week" is provable.
- **Cadence over alarm:** checks run on schedules appropriate to what they watch (hours parity daily; performance weekly; schema on publish). The Engine never pages the customer; urgency is the Strategist's judgment, spoken by the concierge.
- **Internal metrics are allowed and expected** — numbers, thresholds, deltas — and are **never shown to customers as scores** (Law 13). They power sentences.
- **Observation of the platform's own output first:** the published FileMap, the live site, the deploy record. External-surface observation (profiles, reviews, indexing) arrives with the destinations that make it meaningful.
- **Read-only, forever.** The Engine holds no credentials that can write to anything.

## 11 · Presence Monitor · Presence · Studio OS — the ladder of trust

### Presence Monitor (standalone; the doorway)
A business connects the website it already has — WordPress, Webflow, Squarespace, Shopify, Wix, Framer, custom — by URL (public observation) plus optional verification for deeper checks. The Optimization Engine watches it; Presence Intelligence explains what it sees — moments, reviews, recommendations, all in the house grammar. **Monitor never edits anyone's website.** It cannot: it has no write path, and the renderer only renders our own contract.

**Where Monitor ends and Presence begins (the hard line):** Monitor *observes and recommends*; the moment a customer wants the platform to *do* — edit, draft, publish, version, restore — they need a draft that our contract owns. Recommendations in Monitor therefore end in words ("here's what to change, and how, on your platform"), never in buttons that change their site. Presence begins where the Draft Writer begins.

### Presence (the room — built)
Unlocks editing, publishing, versioning, preview, history, restore, templates, the renderer, the pipeline, and AI-assisted editing at the client's chosen rung.

### Studio OS (the practice)
Unlocks CRM, projects, messaging, contracts, billing, automation, and agency tooling. Everything builds upward; nothing above weakens a law below.

**⚠ Commercial reconciliation required — see §15.** Monitor's *architecture* (observation-only, read-only, evidence-based) contradicts nothing and is ratified here. Monitor's *productization* — a fifth commercial offering — touches the frozen Edition Strategy ("two surfaces, one product line," four editions) and the frozen Feature Matrix. That change is proposed as **Amendment 2** below and awaits the owner's word.

## 12 · Presence Health

Law 13 stands untouched: customers receive **sentences, never scores**. Internally, health is modeled: per-dimension states (content, hosting, domain, freshness, discoverability, consistency, reputation — each with thresholds and evidence), combined by *worst-first with judgment* into the tri-state that already exists (healthy / needs_attention / outdated), rendered as one human sentence with at most one detail line. The M7 `deriveHealth` engine is the seed of this model; the Engine's evidence will widen its inputs without ever widening its vocabulary. Internal numbers exist; the customer's language never contains them.

## 13 · Industry Packs

A pack is the complete expression of a vertical, versioned as one unit:

**Pack anatomy:** flagship template (per the frozen one-flagship rule) · content-model shape (how the contract's entities read in this vertical: offerings=menu / services / treatments / listings) · SEO rules (what this vertical must answer) · schema mappings (Restaurant, Dentist, LegalService, …) · AI prompt foundations (Writer/Editor voice constraints per vertical; risk posture — medical/legal write conservatively, per the frozen risk notes) · optimization rules (what the Engine watches extra: menus go stale monthly, contractors seasonally) · Business Moments calendar (the vertical's rhythms and holidays).

**Packs defined for:** Restaurant · Dental · Medical · Legal · Salon · Contractor · Fitness · Retail · Real Estate · Professional Services · Hospitality · Nonprofit.

**Order discipline:** this list defines *architecture*, not sequence. The shipping order remains the frozen vertical strategy (03 §5): restaurants → salons → home services/contractors → fitness → professional services → hospitality → medical/dental → retail(brochure); real estate and nonprofit slot in by the same contract-fit-first rule when evidence justifies them. High-risk verticals (medical, dental, legal) ship only with the mature AI rails the frozen strategy demands.

## 14 · Product Laws — reaffirmed and extended

Reaffirmed verbatim in force: AI never invents facts (11) · every AI action has a manual equivalent (25) · customers own their content (1) and their domains (1) · customers may disable AI completely (24) · no client-facing scores (13) · one draft, one renderer, one pipeline, nothing bypasses (6).

**"AI never publishes automatically" — reconciled precisely:** nothing AI-initiated ever reaches a live surface without either (a) a human pressing the ritual's button, or (b) a human having granted **explicit, revocable, standing consent** with a review window (rung 4 — 03 §3, frozen). Rung 4 is not automation past the customer; it is the customer's own standing instruction, visible, attributed in History, and revocable in one tap. Default is and remains Suggest. Both texts hold; neither is weakened.

**New laws (Part 11 continues):**

30. **Two systems, one direction.** Observation and judgment are separate subsystems forever; evidence flows only from Engine to Intelligence; neither publishes.
31. **No recommendation without evidence.** Every recommendation carries why, evidence, expected benefit, effort, and undoability. "Best practice" alone recommends nothing.
32. **One host.** However many specialists work behind the curtain, the customer hears one concierge, in one voice, in merchant words. Personas are never customer-facing.
33. **Moments, not dashboards.** The primary intelligent experience is a few calm, evidenced sentences a day — capped, deduped, dismissable-forever — never a wall of metrics. Trust always outweighs automation.
