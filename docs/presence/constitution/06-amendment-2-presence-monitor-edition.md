## 1 · One product line, one ladder

Presence Monitor is **not a separate product family**. It is the entry edition of the existing Presence line. The unified progression, frozen:

**Presence Monitor → Presence → Presence Managed → Presence Agency → Presence Enterprise → Studio OS**

The frozen "two surfaces, one product line" principle survives intact: editions one through five *are* the one product line; **Studio OS remains the operator surface** — the summit the ladder opens into when a business becomes a practice that cares for other businesses. The ladder is a ladder of trust: each rung earns the next, and no rung is ever required.

## 2 · Edition Strategy (03 §2, as amended)

| Edition | Who buys it | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| **Presence Monitor** | A business owner keeping the website they already have | The doorway. Connect any existing site — WordPress, Webflow, Squarespace, Shopify, Wix, Framer, custom — and the platform observes, explains, and recommends in the house grammar. It never edits their site: recommendations end in words, never in buttons that change reality. |
| **Presence** | A business owner, directly | The standalone product: the room, the draft, the ritual — editing, publishing, versioning, preview, history, restore, hosting. Presence begins where the Draft Writer begins. |
| **Presence Managed** | A studio, on behalf of its client | The same product wrapped in a studio relationship: human concierge, white-glove care. |
| **Presence Agency** | An agency | Studio OS licensed under the agency's brand + Presence wholesale for its fleet. |
| **Presence Enterprise** | An organization | Presence with governance: locations, roles, approvals, federation, audit. |
| *(Surface)* **Studio OS** | Studios and agencies | The operator platform: CRM, projects, messaging, contracts, billing, automation, agency tooling. Never sold to SMBs; the second surface, not a sixth edition. |

Terminology freeze extended: **"Monitor" is the edition name; "connect your site" is its verb.** "CMS" remains internal-only vocabulary, as always.

## 3 · Feature Matrix (02 Part 2, one column added)

Presence Monitor's column: Business Moments, health sentences, Optimization Engine observation, evidence-backed recommendations, concierge grammar — **✓**. Editing, drafts, publishing, versioning, preview, history, restore, templates, hosting, domains-managed-by-us — **—** (they begin at Presence). Structured content export of *their own observations and record* — ✓ (Law 2 applies to every edition). Runtime plugins / page builders / client-facing scores — **✗** everywhere, unchanged.

## 4 · The additive law (new Product Law 34)

**Every edition is additive, and every upgrade preserves everything.** Moving up the ladder — Monitor to Presence, Presence to Managed, any rung to any higher rung — preserves customer data, content, history, domains, and workflows without exception, without migration loss, and without re-entry. Nothing a customer built, wrote, connected, or configured is ever the price of an upgrade. Moving *down* or *out* is equally protected by the existing ownership laws (1–4): export is a right, leaving is easy, returning is warm. A Monitor customer who adopts Presence keeps their observation history and evidence record — the platform already knows their business the day the room opens.

## 5 · Boundary discipline (restating the hard line)

Monitor observes and recommends; Presence acts. The Optimization Engine gains no write path at any edition. The renderer renders only our contract at every edition. The one draft, one renderer, one pipeline law is edition-independent, and Law 21 extends naturally: **every edition inherits every law unmodified.**
