## 4. Commercial Reconciliation

The ladder — **Monitor → Presence → Presence Managed → Agency → Enterprise** — still makes sense and every rung is real: Monitor (observation, guidance, readiness, foundations-guided) and Presence (hosting, publishing, full CMS/studio) are shipped and edition-gated where it matters (publish/restore 403 for Monitor). **Gap:** within paid editions, entitlement is still binary (full/readonly) — Managed vs Presence vs Enterprise differ operationally (who does the work) but not yet in feature flags, and studio tools aren't gated by rung. That's a router-level gate whenever the pricing call is made (L1). Agency limits (seats/clients) and usage summaries exist and are enforced — billing integration is prepared, not wired. No feature was found in the wrong edition; the one *positioning* note: Migration Readiness is a Monitor feature that sells Presence — keep it free on Monitor forever.

## 5. Customer Journey Review

- **Discovery → Signup:** the missing transition. Everything downstream of a provisioned site is calm and guided; getting the site provisioned is operator-only. (L1.)
- **Domain:** guided plans with exact records — good. Purchase itself leaves the platform (guided registrar steps) until L4.
- **Migration:** connect → verify → readiness → inventory → plan → approve — coherent and honest; content still moves by hands through the CMS (acceptable, stated).
- **Launch:** the Launch Assistant closes this well — one list, derived from reality.
- **Daily management / Optimization / Growth:** the room + moments + two doors ("Look things over", "The technical side"). **Friction note:** the business tab has accreted (identity, hours, monitor card, papers, two door buttons) — recommend grouping the two cards under one "Working with us" strip when next touched. UI-only, safe to delay.
- **Confusion found:** the two email goals (§2.1) — one word to the customer should mean one flow.
- **Agency:** the operating system exists as API + queues; there is **no agency UI surface yet** — agencies would operate through the operator today. Safe to delay past small-business launch; required before Agency-edition GA (L5).
- **Enterprise:** a ladder rung with no distinct feature set yet — leave as positioning until demand shapes it.
- **Leaving:** one button, one file, transfer-out plans. **One ownership gap found:** the export carries media *storage paths*, not fetchable URLs/binaries — the words leave perfectly; the photographs need one more step (L4, small).

## 6. Launch Readiness Assessment

Hosting/SSL: automatic, restorable, tested (30/30 pipeline suite). Domains/DNS: guided plans + desired-state editor + drift/repair — works today with the customer placing records. Email: guided presets + posture checks; the reset/invite email path proven live this week. Migration: reviewable end-to-end. Publishing: the frozen pipeline, byte-tested. Export/Ownership: shipped and verified field-by-field. AI: five tools, deterministic-first, edition-appropriate. Optimization: 27 providers on every run. Agency: API-complete.

**Could a real customer successfully launch today?** **Yes — as a concierge launch** (operator provisions, customer runs the room; this matches the current business). **For public self-serve launch, the remaining blockers, complete list:**
1. Self-serve signup → provisioning (exists as an idempotent admin flow; needs a public front).
2. Billing wired to entitlements (entitlement gate exists; nothing charges).
3. Scheduled observation runs + failure alerting (the intelligence stack runs on operator trigger).
4. A decided legal/support posture for customer-placed DNS mistakes (runbook, not code).
Nothing else blocks. Notably *not* blockers: registrar APIs (guided works), agency UI (concierge-mediated), email service (never the goal).

## 10. Final Questions

**Is the platform internally coherent?** Yes. One duplication (email goals), a handful of small helper/substrate debts, and zero contract conflicts across 35 migrations and 27 providers — coherence has been actively defended (M9.5F/G) rather than assumed.

**Is the roadmap still coherent?** The completed roadmap is; the numbering was not (two "Platform Services", the M9.5 letter tail). The L1–L5 launch track resets it with an honest ordering: commerce, operations, depth, adapters, verticals.

**Can a small business understand the product?** Yes — because they never meet the architecture. They meet: your website, a room to tend it, three moments that matter, two doors ("look things over", "the technical side"), plans they approve in sentences, and one button that hands them everything they own.

**Can an agency understand the product?** Conceptually yes — "the same room, multiplied, with queues instead of tabs" — but today they'd experience it through an operator. The Agency edition is architecture-complete and experience-incomplete (register #11).

**Is customer ownership stronger than when the project began?** Substantially. It began as a principle; it is now executable: the export with the rendered site inside it, transfer-out as guided as transfer-in, the approval law on every infrastructure change, deny-by-default boundaries, and a monitor edition that proves value before asking for anything. The one soft spot is media binaries in the export (register #2) — small, named, scheduled.

**Would you launch this architecture?** Yes — for the concierge launch, today, without reservation: every failure mode found in testing was fixed and re-verified, and rollback exists at every layer. For public self-serve: after L1 and L2, and I would not launch before L2 — an intelligence platform that only observes when a human presses the button is not yet keeping its central promise.

**The five highest-priority milestones remaining:** L1 Signup & Commerce, L2 Scheduled Operations, L3 Optimization Judgment Depth (+ debt paydown), L4 Write-Capable Adapters (+ export media), L5 Verticals & Agency Console — in that order, for the reasons in §3.
