# L3.3 — Optimization Concierge Intelligence

The Concierge already explained optimization recommendations (L3.2). L3.3 makes it a calm optimization *advisor* — able to field the natural follow-up questions a business owner asks, and help them make **business** decisions, not technical ones. Deterministic throughout: no new providers, evidence, recommendation types, dashboards, reports, chat features, or open-ended AI conversations. It stays one grounded host.

## Conversation philosophy

The Concierge is not a chatbot and not an SEO consultant. It is the one host, answering from the grounding pack — the customer's own moments, recommendations, judgments, and evidence — and nothing else. Every answer is assembled deterministically; the same question about the same moment gives the same words. It simplifies but never changes meaning, never invents, and when it doesn't know, it says so. There is no "AI" persona, no personality performance — just plain, steady help.

## Follow-up guidance

A customer can now ask, in their own words, and be understood:

| The owner asks… | Intent | The answer, in spirit |
|---|---|---|
| Why? / Why does this matter? | why_important | In terms of customers, trust, being found, turning a visit into a booking. |
| Why now? / Can I wait? | can_wait | "You can. Waiting costs nothing; it stays here quietly, and you'll hear if it ever gets serious." |
| What happens if I ignore it? | if_ignored | "Nothing breaks, and nobody will chase you." |
| Can Studio OS do this? | can_you_do | Ownership, plainly: ours (silent), or we-prepare-you-approve, or "this part is yours — I'll point you to the spot." |
| Will anything break? | will_break | "Nothing on your live site can break — draft first, publish all at once, every version kept." |
| How long does this take? | how_long | The customer's small part ("a few minutes"), or "no time from you — it's handled on our side." |
| Do I need to know anything technical? | need_technical | "Not at all — everything here is in plain words; anything technical is ours." |
| Will this affect my website? | affects_site | "Only what you choose, and only once you publish." |
| Will this affect my email? | affects_email | Honest: "No, your email is untouched" — or, for foundations, "it can touch your mail settings, but only after you approve." |
| What should I do first? | what_first | Names one concrete starting point (the top moment), "the rest can follow in any order." |
| How important is this vs everything? | how_important_relative | Places it calmly among the others: "the one I'd look at first" or "a touch behind — none of it urgent." |

The classifier is ordered so these specific phrasings win over the generic intents, and the existing intents (why_recommend, what_means, if_ignored, what_changes, do_it, how_fix, overview) are unchanged.

## Decision support (business decisions, never technical)

Value is always explained through business dimensions (`DIM_PHRASE`) — "how easily customers find you when they search," "how much customers trust what they see," "how easily a visit turns into a booking" — never through mechanisms. The Concierge helps the owner weigh *their* choices: which task to start with, how one compares to the rest, whether they can wait. It never asks them to weigh a technical trade-off, because those aren't theirs to make.

## Customer reassurance (reduce uncertainty, never add work)

Three reassurances are reinforced on demand and never contradicted:
- **Nothing breaks.** Draft-first, atomic publish, every version kept; for guided fixes, nothing to domain/email/security until you approve.
- **You don't need to be technical.** Everything is plain words; the technical part is ours.
- **It's not urgent.** Steady improvement at the owner's pace; the platform will speak up, honestly, only if something ever becomes serious.

## Ownership reinforcement

Every answer routes through the same ownership map as the rest of L3.x: **platform-owned** ("ours to handle — taken care of for you"), **guided fix** ("we prepare, you approve, nothing until you say yes"), **customer** ("this part is yours — I'll take you right to the spot"), always with the **approval boundary** ("nothing changes on your live site until you've published it yourself") and a **manual alternative** implied by pointing at the room. The Concierge never hands the owner a technical artifact to paste.

## Conversation boundaries

- **Deterministic and grounded** — no free-form generation; answers come from the grounding pack.
- **Honest refusal** — "I can only speak to what's actually been measured about your presence" when there's no grounding.
- **One host** — no personas, no "AI/assistant/chatbot" language, no exclamation marks (tone-lint enforced).
- **Opt-in depth** — the moment is a calm one-liner; the follow-up answers are there for whoever asks, never pushed.
- **No new surface** — this milestone adds intents to an existing pure function; it builds no chat feature and opens no open-ended conversation.

## Real-world validation

Full conversations were run against real websites: a nine-turn thread on a single moment produced **nine distinct answers**, all calm, plain, consistent (no contradictions — "this part is yours" alongside "your email is untouched" alongside "nothing breaks"), with **no jargon and no repetition**. It reads like a thoughtful advisor the owner would actually enjoy asking.
