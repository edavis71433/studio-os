# Davis Digital Studio — Website Master Plan

**2026-07-10.** The single source of truth for the DDS website before implementation. Derived from `PHASE-3A-WEBSITE-AUDIT.md`. Governs every page redesign (starting with the homepage) so the whole site shares one strategy, voice, and experience. **Pair this with `MESSAGING-GUIDE.md` (voice/vocabulary/secrecy) — that guide is binding on all copy.**

**North-star positioning:** *Davis Digital Studio is a premium, strategy-led studio where one person — Eric — designs your site, runs your SEO, and stays on afterward to keep it working. Working with him feels calm, organized, and personal, and you can see everything happening in one private space.* We sell **outcomes and a relationship**, never software.

---

## 1. Navigation architecture
**Primary nav (one canonical set, every page):**
`How we work` · `The experience` · `Services ▾` (Web design · SEO strategy · Growth Partnership) · `Industries` · `Results` · `About` · **`Book a free call`** (button)
- Add **Results** to the top nav (proof must not be footer-only).
- Services dropdown labels must match the canonical offer names (Web design / SEO strategy / **Growth Partnership** — never "Ongoing care").
- One primary CTA button in the nav: **Book a free call**.

**Footer (one canonical set):**
- *Studio*: How we work · Services · Industries · Results · About
- *Free tools*: **Free site review** (→ audit) · **Free tools** (→ tools hub) — two distinct labels, never both "Free tools".
- *Company*: Contact · Privacy · Terms · Accessibility
- One canonical NAP (resolve Burbank vs Los Angeles) + real social `sameAs` links.

**Fence rule:** no public marketing page may link into `help.html`, `pricing.html`, `signup.html`, or `studio-os-demo.html` (all `noindex`, all part of the platform-behind-the-curtain surface).

## 2. Information architecture
Three journeys, one destination (Book a free call):
- **Decide-fast:** Home → Services → (Web design | SEO | Growth Partnership) → Contact.
- **Build-trust:** Home → How we work → The experience → Results → About → Contact.
- **Self-serve/curious:** Home/Tools → a free tool → (captured lead) → Contact.
Every page is ≤2 clicks from Book-a-call and from at least one proof surface (Results/testimonial).

## 3. Messaging hierarchy (per page, top to bottom)
1. **Outcome headline** (what the client gets — not what we do).
2. **One-sentence "why us"** (the calm, personal, stays-after-launch difference).
3. **Proof** (testimonial / result / credential — as early as the layout allows).
4. **The substance** (how-we-help, in plain language, benefit-framed).
5. **The experience** (what working together feels like — portal-as-benefit).
6. **One clear next step** (Book a free call).
Homepage order specifically: hero → proof band → the two-part promise → the client experience (with screenshot) → who-it's-for → founder trust → final CTA.

## 4. Brand voice
Calm, confident, honest, first-person (Eric). A trusted expert who under-promises and over-delivers, never hypes, and would rather tell you the truth than close the sale. (Full definition + do/don't in `MESSAGING-GUIDE.md`.) **This voice is the brand's moat — protect it in every rewrite.**

## 5. Tone guidelines
- **Warm but not casual**; **confident but not boastful**; **plain but not dumbed-down**.
- Lead with the client's gain, not our process. Name real feelings ("you always know where things stand").
- No exclamation-mark hype, no jargon, no fabricated numbers, no fear-selling.
- Premium = restraint. Say less, mean more.

## 6. CTA strategy
- **One primary conversion goal site-wide: Book a free call.** One button style, one verb.
- Free tools are top-of-funnel; each ends with a single benefit-led step toward a call.
- **Reduce competing CTAs:** homepage first screen = one primary + one secondary (not four); tools hub = one recommended "start here" path + secondary options.
- Standardize repeated phrases to one destination ("See what your site needs" → the free site review, everywhere).
- Secondary CTAs are text links, not competing buttons.

## 7. Trust strategy (the highest-ROI program)
- **Do not fabricate; do not abandon the honesty positioning.** Add the proof categories that don't need a big portfolio:
  1. **Testimonials** (2–4, permissioned) — themes: reliability, calm experience, results.
  2. **One case study** (situation → fixed-first → the choice → result), templated.
  3. **Anonymized/composite outcomes** now ("a local service business, now on page one…").
  4. **Specific credentials** (M.S. Business Analytics CSUN; enterprise background; 9+ years) — and add craft credibility (web/SEO).
  5. **A written promise/standard** ("you hear back within one business day"; "credited toward any project").
- **Place proof everywhere it's earned:** homepage proof band, the-experience (a testimonial — highest leverage), contact + checkout (credential strip + a face), work→Results, monthly-retainer (long-term client quote).
- Fix the **inverted trust** problem: the credential strip that lives on free-tool pages must also live where money/commitment happen.

## 8. Screenshot strategy (portal, as "your private client space")
- Show, don't just assert, the client experience — but tastefully (small, cropped, elegant), never as a software tour.
- Capture real, clean-data shots of: **approvals**, **invoices/billing**, **files**, **progress/timeline**, **plain-English report**.
- Placement: the-experience (2–3, mapped to beats) · how-we-work step 04 (progress) · services + web-design (portal home) · seo-strategy (report) · contact + checkout (small "how it feels" preview).
- **Rule:** clean, real, on-brand data only — no lorem-ipsum, no placeholder names.

## 9. Photography strategy
- **Human/photographic register for trust pages** (about portrait, how-we-work "Eric at work"). Keep — do not replace with product shots.
- Add a **real face (Eric)** near the contact and checkout CTAs.
- Real work imagery (before/after, live-site thumbnail) on web-design/work/industries the moment permissioned work exists — never stock as a substitute.
- Consistent treatment: warm, real, unstaged; matches the brand's honesty.

## 10. Portal positioning strategy (secrecy-critical)
- The portal is a **benefit of hiring DDS**, never a product. Frame as *"how it feels to work with me"* — track progress, approve work, see invoices, find files, get reports, request changes.
- **Never** name it as a product, never explain how it's built, never imply software purchase. (Soften existing: "I built a proper client portal" → "every project gets its own private space"; the "Client Portal ↗" chip → a benefit label.)
- Add the currently-missing **effortless-changes / AI-assisted-updates** benefit as a *reason to hire* — as outcome only: *"Need a change later? You just ask, and it happens."* (See Messaging Guide for the exact framing of AI.)

## 11. Page hierarchy (priority + role)
- **Tier 1 (convert):** Home, Services, Contact, the three service pages, Book-a-call.
- **Tier 2 (build trust):** The experience, How we work, About, **Results (new/expanded)**.
- **Tier 3 (SEO/rank):** Industries + 5 industry pages, the free tools + hub.
- **Tier 4 (support/legal):** FAQ (new), what-happens-after (new), privacy/terms/accessibility.
- **Behind the curtain (noindex, unlinked from marketing):** help, pricing, signup, studio-os-demo, the app itself.

## 12. Internal linking strategy
- Every service page links to the relevant **industry** pages and to **Results** + **Growth Partnership**.
- Every **industry** page links (in body) to the service pages, the hub, and one relevant case study — the biggest current SEO miss.
- Every free-tool result links to the matching service page + Book-a-call.
- Home links to all Tier-1/2 pages; Results links out to service + industry pages.
- One canonical anchor per destination (no "Free tools"/"Free site review" ambiguity).

## 13. SEO strategy
- **Canonical names** in titles (Services; Growth Partnership) to align title ↔ nav ↔ URL.
- **Schema:** add BreadcrumbList (services/industries/industry pages), FAQPage (service + industry + engagement FAQ), ItemList (industries hub), and a site-wide **Organization/LocalBusiness** node (logo, `sameAs`, areaServed, founder) — currently everything is `Person`.
- **Industry pages:** unique FAQ content + internal links = the ranking lift; kill the near-duplicate boilerplate.
- **Fix shipping bugs:** OG double-escape (`&amp;amp;`), stale audit-tier JSON-LD, NAP inconsistency.
- **Measurement parity:** GA/consent/SW on all marketing pages (currently missing on the highest-intent ones).
- Keep the OG/Twitter/favicon/theme-color parity added this session; every new page inherits it.

## 14. Conversion strategy
- 🔴 **Capture a lead on every tool** — one consistent model: **instant on-screen value, then one optional benefit-framed email step** that routes answers + email into the CRM as a qualified lead (ROI/estimator/local-visibility capture nothing today).
- De-friction the free Site Score (run on URL alone; gate only the full report).
- Proof + a face at every decision point; portal-as-benefit previewed on conversion pages.
- One recommended path per hub; conservative (credible) defaults on calculators.
- Best-in-class thank-you states everywhere (model on contact's "what happens now"); add a **"what happens after you book"** surface.

## 15. Mobile strategy
- 44px+ targets; no horizontal overflow on tool result cards; first-screen CTAs uncramped.
- Async font-load (`preload`+`onload`) site-wide (8 pages still render-blocking) for fast first paint.
- WebP screenshots with width/height (CLS); mobile `<picture>` sources for all product shots.
- Verify industry feature-boxes and multi-column bands stack cleanly.

## 16. Accessibility strategy
- Baseline already lifted this session (keyboard-operability + focus-trap enhancer, announced results/errors, expanded statement).
- Per-page in redesign: expose meaningful `aria-hidden` marketing copy (homepage reply card); fix low-contrast fine print (contact hint `rgba(255,255,255,.35)`, dark-panel `.4/.6`); inline announced form errors (replace `start.html` `alert()`); keyboard-operable nav dropdown with live `aria-expanded`; visible focus; reduced-motion respected; every image real alt.
- Target: WCAG 2.1 AA (as the accessibility statement now promises).

## 17. Visual consistency strategy
- **One design system, every page:** Fraunces (display) + Inter (body) + Geist Mono (labels); the studio's warm palette + plum `#5b3fa0` accent; consistent spacing, cards, buttons.
- **Re-skin off-brand pages** (`start.html` uses DM Sans/DM Serif; `buy-audit` is standalone) to the shared system — the first post-booking touch must feel like the same company.
- **Extract shared nav/footer/reveal-script into one include/partial** — kills the drift that already exists (naming, GA presence, charset casing) and makes canonical changes one edit, not ten.
- One CTA button style, one link style, one image treatment, one screenshot frame.
- OG image + favicon + theme-color consistent site-wide.

---

**Redesign order (per the audit's ROI ranking):** homepage first (sets the system), then the three service pages, then the-experience/how-we-work/about, then Results + the industry-page rebuild, then the conversion/tool lead-capture fixes, then the new FAQ / what-happens-after pages, then polish. Each page must conform to this plan + the Messaging Guide before it ships.
