# Davis Digital Studio — Launch Checklist

Run every single line before you announce a launch. Launch is the most visible
moment you have, and a broken form or missing redirect going live is exactly what
makes a studio look amateur. This turns launch from a risk into a non-event.

Keep a copy per project so you have a record of what was checked.

Project: [Business Name] · URL: [URL] · Launch date: [DATE]

## Before flipping it live
- [ ] Every page loads and looks right on desktop
- [ ] Every page loads and looks right on mobile (test on a real phone, not just resize)
- [ ] Contact form submits AND the submission actually arrives in your inbox (test it)
- [ ] Any booking/ordering/payment links go to the right place and work
- [ ] All navigation links work, no dead links
- [ ] Phone numbers and email addresses are clickable and correct
- [ ] Business hours, address, and contact info are correct
- [ ] Spelling and grammar pass on every page (read it once, out loud)
- [ ] Images are sized/compressed (no giant files slowing the page)
- [ ] Favicon shows in the browser tab

## SEO and discoverability
- [ ] Every page has a unique title and meta description
- [ ] Open Graph / social preview image set (so shared links look right)
- [ ] sitemap.xml present and submitted to Google Search Console
- [ ] robots.txt is NOT blocking the site (this is a classic launch killer)
- [ ] Google Analytics 4 is installed and firing (check Realtime while you load a page)
- [ ] Google Search Console verified
- [ ] If replacing an old site: 301 redirects from old URLs to new ones are in place

## Technical
- [ ] HTTPS works and the padlock shows (no mixed-content warnings)
- [ ] www and non-www both resolve to the same place
- [ ] 404 page exists and is branded
- [ ] DNS TTL planned if you're cutting over a live domain (lower it ahead of time)
- [ ] Site speed checked (run your own speed-test tool on it)

## After it's live
- [ ] Load the live URL fresh (incognito) and click through once more
- [ ] Add the site to uptime monitoring (UptimeRobot free tier)
- [ ] Capture an SEO baseline in Semrush so you can show progress later
- [ ] Take/record the client walkthrough video
- [ ] Take a backup / snapshot of the final state
- [ ] Confirm final invoice is paid
- [ ] Send the launch announcement email
- [ ] Log the project in your client infrastructure register (domain owner, renewal, host)

## 3 to 5 days later
- [ ] Send the testimonial + Google review request (while they're still thrilled)
