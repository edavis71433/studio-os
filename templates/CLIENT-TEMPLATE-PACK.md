matched to how you already write. Order follows a real project start to finish.

change a price or a policy, change it here too so these never contradict the site.

## 3. CONTENT CHECKLIST (attach to kickoff; one per industry below)

Send these to your one upload spot in the portal. If you're missing something, tell
me and we'll figure out a plan, don't let it stall you.

### Universal (every project)
- [ ] Logo files (the highest-quality versions you have: .svg, .ai, .eps, or large .png)
- [ ] Brand colors and fonts, if you have them
- [ ] Business name exactly as it should appear, plus any tagline
- [ ] Phone, email, address, and hours
- [ ] Links to all social profiles
- [ ] A short sentence on what makes you different from competitors
- [ ] Any existing photos you want used (originals, not screenshots)

### Restaurant / bar / cafe
- [ ] Current menu (PDF or doc, with prices if they should show)
- [ ] Food and drink photos, or permission to shoot them
- [ ] Reservation or ordering link, if any
- [ ] Private events / catering info, if offered

### Home services (plumbing, HVAC, contractors, etc.)
- [ ] Full list of services with short descriptions
- [ ] Service area (cities or zip codes you cover)
- [ ] Licenses, certifications, insurance you want shown
- [ ] Before/after photos of past work
- [ ] Any reviews you want featured

### Salon / spa / beauty
- [ ] Service menu with prices
- [ ] Booking link or system you use
- [ ] Photos of the space and of work (styles, results)
- [ ] Staff names and bios, if you want a team page

### Retail / shop
- [ ] Product list or categories
- [ ] Product photos
- [ ] Whether you sell online now or want to later
- [ ] Store hours and location details

### Professional / office (legal, medical, consulting)
- [ ] Services or practice areas
- [ ] Bios and headshots for each person you want listed
- [ ] Credentials, affiliations, memberships
- [ ] Any intake form or booking process to link
