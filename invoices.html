Export for your bookkeeper

One zip of spreadsheets. QuickBooks Online, Xero, Google Sheets and Excel all read them.

  • invoices.csvEvery invoice sent in those days, one row per line: number, customer, dates and amounts.QuickBooks Online: Settings, Import data, Invoices
  • payments.csvEvery payment that came in: the day, who paid, which invoice, how, and the amount. Retainer payments included.For matching what came into the bank
  • payouts.csvEach deposit Stripe sent you, with what made it up. payments.csv then shows Stripe’s fee on each payment, what you kept and the day it was paid out.For matching your bank statement

Nothing is sent anywhere. Only this download.
