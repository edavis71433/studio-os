What this free website audit checks

This free website audit is a quick SEO analysis of your homepage and up to 7 more pages, picked from your menu, footer and sitemap. It runs more than 30 checks in four areas and puts what to fix first at the top. Many of these checks are what people call technical SEO: the setup behind your pages that helps Google find them, read them and trust them.

Performance

We run Google Lighthouse, Google’s own speed test, on your homepage the way a phone loads it. That includes Core Web Vitals, Google’s measures of how fast the main content appears, how much the page jumps around while loading, and how quickly it responds. We also flag photos that are bigger than they need to be.

Search

Page titles and descriptions (the words Google shows in search results), one clear main heading per page, image descriptions, whether Google is allowed to list each page, your sitemap and robots.txt file (the files that guide Google around your site), structured data (code that tells Google your name, address, phone and hours), pages with very little text, leftover template text, broken internal links (links from one of your pages to another that lead nowhere), and whether the site is set up for phones.

Trust and conversion

A secure connection, a phone number people can tap to call, an email address or contact form, your address, a contact page, a privacy policy, links to your social profiles, a clear button to book, call, order or get a quote on the first screen of your homepage, and how your link looks when someone shares it.

Local

Whether your city appears in your homepage title, main heading or opening text, your address in the footer, a map on the site, and whether your name, address and phone number match across your pages. Add your city in the form to get the most from this part.

How it works

  1. Enter your website. Type your address, pick your type of business, and add your city if you serve a local area.
