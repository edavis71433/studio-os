How Studio OS works

Plain answers to the things people ask most — and a real person when you need one.

Getting around

The bar at the top is the same everywhere — your pages, search (press Ctrl K, or ⌘K on a Mac), what needs a look, and your account. What you see adapts to your plan and role; anything you don't have simply isn't shown, so there are no dead ends.

Working with your studio

Your project, in one place.

When a studio builds for you, your project lives in your updates page — progress, files to review, things waiting on your OK, messages, and invoices. Anything that needs you also lands in your email, so nothing depends on you checking in. Proposals and agreements arrive as a link you can read, sign, and (when there's a deposit) pay — all in one sitting, and a signed copy comes straight back to your inbox.

Publishing & approval

Nothing goes live without your OK.

You edit a draft, preview it, then publish with one calm step. Every publish is kept as a version — if anything looks wrong, you can restore an earlier one. Your live site never changes until you publish, and a failed publish leaves it untouched.

Your data & ownership

It's yours, always.

You can export everything you own at any time, connect or disconnect services freely, and leave without penalty. We store business facts and text, never your customers' card details (billing is handled by Stripe).

Connected services

Connect the tools you already use to read your own data — safely, with only the access you approve. Any change we'd make on your behalf is proposed first and waits for your approval. You can disconnect at any time and your accounts stay exactly as they are.

Your plan & billing

Your plan decides which features you see. Upgrading adds capabilities — nothing resets and no data is lost. Downgrading simply hides what you're not using; your information is preserved and comes back if you upgrade again. Manage your subscription, payment method, or cancel any time from your billing portal.

Reliability & your website's uptime

Your published website is served as plain, fast pages from a global content network — it keeps working even if our platform is having a bad day. Every version you publish is kept forever and restorable in one click, so a mistake is never more than a minute from fixed. Publishing changes nothing until it succeeds; there are no maintenance windows and your site never goes down for updates.

The workspace itself runs on infrastructure with independently published uptime commitments and security audits (SOC 2). If we're ever the reason something's down, we'll say so plainly and fix it.
