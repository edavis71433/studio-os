Running your clients’ sitesClient sites›

Every client’s website is a card on Sites. + New site makes one from a business name and, if you like, a template or a starter kit. Nobody is emailed until you press Invite the owner on its card, so you can build in private. Pin keeps a site at the top of your list.

Duplicate starts a new site from one you already built, pages and photos included. Whatever came across waits for you to change it, or to press Keep on the new site’s card, before it can go live. Save as a template keeps a site as a starting point; the Templates tab lists your templates and starter kits, where you can rename or remove them. The Domains tab lists every domain and when it renews.

Inside a client, Switch site on the card at the top of the menu jumps to another one. Move to trash takes a site offline and keeps it in the Trash at the foot of Sites for 90 days, where Restore brings it back. In the editor, Versions keeps the pages you deleted for 30 days, each with Put back.

Before you build, Ask for their content on a site’s ⋯ menu sends the owner a short form for their words and photos. It needs no sign-in, saves as they type, and their photos land in that site’s Files. When it’s theirs, the Hand-over kit puts who to call, short how-tos, video links and files at the top of Help in their portal. Plan my week, on Home and Sites, lists every client in the order they need you over the next seven days.

Emailing someone from their recordCustomers and deals›

Email anyone you have an address for, with or without a deal or a project. On their record press Email, or pick the Email tab above their timeline. On Pipeline, open a deal and press Email. Write a subject and your message, or start from a saved reply.

It goes out as your studio and stays on their timeline, and when they answer, the reply comes back to your Inbox. If an earlier email to them bounced or was marked as spam, the box tells you instead of sending.

To-dos, invoices, and getting something backCustomers and deals›

To-dos lists everything you have to do, for anyone: a person, a customer, a deal, or just you. It groups them by when they’re due, and Home shows the next few. Log a call on someone’s record and “Follow up on” adds the to-do in the same step.

Invoices shows every invoice you’ve sent, with what’s owed to you, what’s overdue and what came in this month. From any row you can open the deal, download the PDF or record a payment by check, cash, transfer or Zelle.

Deleted something by mistake? Undo, in the message that follows, brings it straight back. After that, Recently deleted (in Settings) keeps deals, contacts and to-dos for 30 days, each with Restore.

Updates and estimatesCustomers and deals›

Send an update, from the Inbox, writes one message to many customers at once: new hours, a holiday, an offer. You pick who gets it and see it before it goes.

An estimate is a quick price before a proposal. Send one from a deal; the customer approves it or says not now, and an approved estimate becomes a proposal or an invoice in one step. A recurring invoice, started from a deal, goes out on its own every month, every three months or every year.

Your data, and who owns itYour account›

It’s yours, always. You can export everything you own at any time, connect or disconnect services freely, and leave without penalty. We store business facts and text, never your customers’ card details (billing is handled by Stripe).

What connecting a service doesConnections›

Connect the tools you already use to read your own data — safely, with only the access you approve. Any change we’d make on your behalf is proposed first and waits for your approval. You can disconnect at any time and your accounts stay exactly as they are. See your connections.

Your planYour account›

Your plan decides which features you see. Upgrading adds capabilities — nothing resets and no data is lost. Downgrading simply hides what you’re not using; your information is preserved and comes back if you upgrade again. Manage your subscription, payment method, or cancel any time from your billing portal.

It’s in Settings, under Your plan, for the account’s owner.

Reliability and your website’s uptimeYour website›

Your published website is served as plain, fast pages from a global content network — it keeps working even if our platform is having a bad day. Every version you publish is kept and restorable in one click, so a mistake is never more than a minute from fixed. Publishing changes nothing until it succeeds; there are no maintenance windows and your site never goes down for updates.

The workspace itself runs on infrastructure with independently published uptime commitments and security audits (SOC 2). If we’re ever the reason something’s down, we’ll say so plainly and fix it.
