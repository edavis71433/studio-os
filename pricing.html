What changes the number

Four things, mostly.

How many pages actually earn their place

A focused five-page site that converts beats a twenty-page site nobody finishes reading. I'd rather build you the first one.

Whether the words already exist

If you have copy and photos that work, we use them. If you don't, writing them is real work and I'll say so up front rather than discover it halfway.

What has to connect to what

Online booking, online ordering, a menu that changes weekly, a customer login — each one is a decision with a cost attached, and you'll hear it before you commit to it.

How far behind search has left you

Sometimes the site is fine and the problem is that nobody can find it. That's a smaller job than a rebuild, and I'll tell you when that's the case.

What I won't do is quote you a number before I understand the business, or let you buy something bigger than you need. The call is free, it's me on the other end, and you'll leave it knowing what this costs whether or not you hire me.
