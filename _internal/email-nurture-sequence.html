The "last email" line dramatically increases open rates because it feels respectful. The soft CTA (free call) converts better at this stage than pushing the paid product.
How to set this up:
1. Create a free account at kit.com (formerly ConvertKit)
2. Create a new Sequence with these 3 emails
3. Set delays: Email 1 = immediately, Email 2 = 3 days, Email 3 = 7 days
4. Add a subscribe action to your Formspree form webhook, or manually add leads to the sequence after each report card submission
5. Replace {{business_name}}, {{first_name}}, {{business_type}}, {{city}} with merge tags from your email platform
