What can change this number

A ballpark is honest about being a ballpark. What moves the real number — up or down — is usually one of these, and it's exactly what a discovery call is for:

  • • How much content is ready. Finished copy and photos speed things up; needing them written or shot adds scope.
  • • How custom the design needs to be. Applying a brand you already have vs. building one from scratch.
  • • Special functionality. Booking, e-commerce, or a custom tool built around how your business actually runs.
  • • Timeline. A tight deadline compresses the work and can add a rush fee; a flexible one keeps costs lower.

Every business is different — that's the point. On a short call I turn this range into a confirmed number in writing, with no surprises after.
