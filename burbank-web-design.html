Why local is a different job

Ranking in Burbank isn’t the same as ranking.

Most web designers build you a nice site and call the search part done. But a Burbank business doesn’t need to beat the whole internet — it needs to beat the eleven other places within driving distance that do the same thing. That is a much smaller, much more winnable fight, and it is won on details most sites never bother with.

Your Google Business Profile has to say the same thing your website says, down to the punctuation. Your hours have to be right, everywhere, including the holidays. Your pages have to name the neighborhoods you actually serve instead of hiding behind “the greater Los Angeles area.” And the site has to load fast on a phone, on a spotty connection, in a parking lot — because that is where local searches genuinely happen.

None of that is glamorous. All of it is the difference between showing up in the map results and being the business nobody can find.

Questions I actually get asked

Straight answers.

Do you actually work with Burbank businesses in person?

Yes. I'm based in Burbank, and for local clients I'd rather start with a real conversation than a form. If it's easier to meet than to schedule a video call, we meet.

How long does a website take?

A full custom build usually runs four to six weeks, depending on how quickly photos and content come together. Smaller projects move faster. I'll give you a realistic date on the first call rather than an optimistic one I'd have to walk back later.

Can you get me to the top of Google for my business?

Nobody can promise a ranking, and you should be careful with anyone who does. What I can tell you is that local searches in Burbank are far less competitive than national ones, that most local sites do the basics badly, and that fixing the basics is usually where the movement comes from.

What does it cost?

It depends on how many pages earn their place, whether the words and photos already exist, and what has to connect to what. I quote a real number on a free call once I understand the business, instead of publishing a range that would be wrong for most people reading it.

Do you disappear after the site launches?

No, and that's most of the point. A Growth Partnership keeps the same person on your site every month, so it keeps up with your business instead of slowly falling behind. You can cancel anytime and the site is yours either way.
