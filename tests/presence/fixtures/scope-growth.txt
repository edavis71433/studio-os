7. PROCESS AND TIMELINE

The estimate below assumes content arrives on schedule. It is a plan, not a guarantee.

Kickoff — Agreement signed, deposit received, questionnaire and checklist returned, domain transfer started. Week 1.

Content — You send content and photography. I review it and flag gaps. Weeks 1 to 2.

Build — Site built on a private preview link. Your current site stays live and untouched. Weeks 2 to 5.

Launch — Final approval, balance paid, domain pointed to the new site, everything verified. Week 6 or 7.

Handoff — Walkthrough video delivered, old site cancelled once stable. Week 7.

Reporting — Three monthly traffic and lead reports. Months 1 to 3 after launch.

9. LAUNCH, ACCEPTANCE, AND SUPPORT

The site is considered accepted when it goes live with your approval. For fourteen days after launch I will fix, at no charge, anything that is broken or does not work as described in this document. Broken means broken. It does not mean new requests or changes of mind, which are handled as change orders.

After that fourteen day window, the site is yours to run. The walkthrough video shows you how to update your content yourself. If you would rather I handle ongoing updates, SEO, and reporting, that is a separate monthly arrangement and we can talk about it whenever you are ready.

10. ABOUT SEARCH RESULTS

Worth stating plainly, in writing, before we start.

The site will be significantly better than the current one on day one. It will be faster, readable by Google, correctly structured, and built around real keywords. That part is immediate.

Actually climbing in search results is not immediate. Realistically it takes about six months before you see a meaningful change in how much traffic search brings you, and that assumes the site stays current and your Google Business Profile stays accurate. Anyone who promises you faster than that is not being straight with you.
