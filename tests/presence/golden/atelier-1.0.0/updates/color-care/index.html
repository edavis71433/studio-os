Caring for color between visits

By Maria Lopez · Feb 10, 2026

Fresh color fades fastest in its first two weeks.

Keep it bright

  • Wash in cool water
  • Use a sulfate-free shampoo
  • Gloss every six weeks

Ask about a gloss when you book your next visit.
