Good to know

Do you charge for consultations?

No. Color consultations are free, in person or by sending us a few photos.

What is your cancellation policy?

Please give us 24 hours' notice. Late cancellations are charged half the service.

Do you travel for weddings?

Yes, anywhere within 30 miles of the studio, with a travel fee beyond that.
