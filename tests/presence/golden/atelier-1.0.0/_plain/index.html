About us

Good to know

Do you take walk-ins?

Always at the bar, and we keep a third of the patio unreserved. Parties of 5+ should book ahead.

Is the patio dog-friendly?

Yes — water bowls provided, treats negotiable.

Do you have vegetarian options?

About half the menu, and the kitchen is happy to adapt most plates. Just ask your server.
