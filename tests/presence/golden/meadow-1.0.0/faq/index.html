Frequently asked questions

Do you deliver the same day?

Yes, for orders placed by 1pm, Monday to Saturday, within our delivery area.

Can I choose the flowers?

Tell us the colors and the mood, and we pick the freshest flowers that fit. For one particular flower, call us first.

How far ahead should we book wedding flowers?

Three to six months is ideal. We take a small number of weddings each weekend.
