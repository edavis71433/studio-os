About Larkspur Tax & Bookkeeping

A small accounting office that does your taxes, keeps your books and answers the phone. Flat prices quoted before we start, and meetings in person or by video.

Dana Whitfield opened the office in 2011 after twelve years at a downtown firm, with one desk and a promise to return every call the same day.

Today a team of five looks after about 400 households and 120 small businesses, from food trucks to dental offices, and we still answer our own phone.

Where we work

Burbank, Glendale and the east San Fernando Valley
