Good to know

What should I bring to the first meeting?

Last year's return, this year's W-2s and 1099s, and any letters from the IRS or the state. We send a checklist when you book.

Can we meet by video?

Yes. About half our clients never visit the office, and you can send documents through a secure link.

Do you work with people outside Burbank?

Yes, anywhere in California, and we file in most other states for clients who moved.
