Good to know

I'm a complete beginner. Is that OK?

Yes. Every class has beginners in it, and the coaches scale each round to where you are.

What should I bring?

Water, a towel, and hand wraps if you have them. We lend gloves and wraps for your first visits.

Is the first class really free?

Yes. Book any class online and tell the coach it is your first time.
