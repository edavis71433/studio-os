About Hale & Morrow

A small Pasadena firm that writes wills and trusts, guides families through probate, and handles divorce and custody with care. Plain answers, and flat fees where we can.

Eleanor Hale and James Morrow met as public defenders and opened the firm in 2014, above a bookshop on Colorado Boulevard.

We keep the practice small on purpose: you talk to the lawyer handling your matter, not an assistant, and we return calls within a day.

Where we work

Pasadena, Altadena, South Pasadena and the San Gabriel Valley
