Good to know

What does a first consultation cost?

Estate planning consultations are free. Family law consultations are $250 for an hour, credited to your fees if you hire us.

Do I need a trust, or just a will?

In California a trust usually keeps your family out of probate court. We will tell you plainly if a will is enough.

Can we meet by video?

Yes. Documents can be signed in our office, or with a mobile notary at your home.
