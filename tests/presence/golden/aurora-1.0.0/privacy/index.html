Privacy policy

Effective 2026-07-01

Who we are

This website belongs to Vermilion & Vine. You can reach us at: 5119 York Boulevard, Los Angeles, CA 90042 · (323) 555-0142 · hello@vermilionandvine.example.

What this website collects

This website has no forms and collects nothing you type.

Reviews

The reviews on this website come from our customers. After a visit we may email you to ask for one, and every such email has a link to stop them. If you leave a review, we keep your name, your star rating, what you wrote and any email address you give. Nothing appears here until we have read it and said yes; then your rating, your words, the name you gave and the date show on this website, sometimes with a reply from us. Your email address is never shown, and if you ask, we’ll take your review down.

Cookies and visit counts

This website sets no cookies and stores nothing on your device, which is why there is no cookie banner.

It counts its own visits, without any outside analytics or advertising company. When a page opens, it notes which page, the name of the website you came from, any campaign tag in the link, and your kind of device, browser and country. When you tap a phone number, an email address, a button, a download or a link to another website, it notes which kind of link it was and, for a button, a phone number or an email address, the words on it. When you leave a page, it notes how long the page was open. It never keeps your IP address; it keeps a scrambled code made from it that changes every day, so a visit can be counted once without saying who you are. If your browser asks websites not to track you, nothing is counted. Only Vermilion & Vine and the studio that runs this website see these counts, and they are never sold or shared for advertising.

Hosting

The site is served as static pages by our hosting provider, which may keep standard, short-lived server logs (such as IP addresses) for security and reliability.

Your choices

If you’ve sent us a message or left a review and would like to see, correct, or delete it, contact us using the details above and we’ll take care of it.
