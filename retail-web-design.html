Common questions

Questions I hear from retail & boutiques.

Do I need a full online store?

Not always. Sometimes the smarter move is a beautiful, findable site that drives people into your shop, with online selling added only where it actually pays off. I'll tell you honestly which one fits your business — even if it's the smaller option.

Can it work with the point-of-sale or inventory I already use?

Yes. I build around the tools you already have rather than forcing a rebuild, so your site and your shop stay in sync without doubling your work.

How do I compete with the big online retailers?

By being the obvious local choice — strong local SEO, a clear brand story, and the things a big box can't offer: your curation, your people, your neighborhood. Your site is built to make those stand out.
