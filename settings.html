    Automations

    Ready-made recipes, and the switches behind them. Each saves as you flip it.
    Acknowledge new enquiries within a minute
    Someone who writes in through your website gets one short reply right away, on your brand, with a copy of what they wrote. You still answer them yourself. A new website starts with this on.
    What they’ll readThanks for getting in touch — we’ve got your message and will reply within one business day.…then a copy of what they wrote.
    Follow up quiet enquiries after and days
    Two short emails in your name if they go quiet. They stop the moment they write back, book, or you mark the enquiry in your Inbox (Not a fit, or made a deal) or press Stop follow-ups there. Each one has an unsubscribe link.
    Remind unpaid invoices days after they’re due, at most once a day
    A week before the due date, on the due date, and once more 2 days after — three reminders at most, with the same secure payment link, to the deal’s contact. An invoice with no due date counts as due a week after it was sent.
    What they’ll readJust a friendly nudge: the invoice for $1,250.00 is still open. The same secure link works whenever you’re ready — nothing expires.
    Then one more, a week later
    Two reminders a week apart once it’s overdue. That one is the last; nothing else goes on its own.
    Remind them a week before a proposal or estimate runs out
    A proposal is good for 30 days after you send it, an estimate until the date you set. One note with a fresh link, never on the day another reminder went, and never for a proposal you replaced.
    What they’ll readA quick note: your proposal for Website refresh is good until December 11, a week from now. Here’s the link if you’d like another look.
    Nudge me when a deal has had no next step for days
    An open deal with no edit, no logged contact and nothing scheduled for that long rings your bell and emails you — once a week while it stays quiet.
    What you’ll readThis deal has had no next step for 3 days — a quick follow-up keeps it moving.
    Ask for a review days after a project is done
    When you mark a project complete, its client gets one email 3 days later with a link to your review page — the same email as a request you send by hand. Once per project. Every client is asked, whatever they said in the quick survey: asking only happy clients breaks Google’s rules on reviews.
    What they’ll readThanks for choosing us. If you have a moment, we’d love a quick review — it really helps.
