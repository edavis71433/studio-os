How we work

Here's exactly what happens after you hire me.

Good web design starts with understanding your business, not picking colors. From our first call through launch and beyond, here's what to expect at every step — clear, organized, and built so nothing slips through the cracks.

Most designers run your project out of their inbox. I don't. Whatever step we're on below, you'll never have to ask where things stand, dig for a file, or wonder what's next — it's all laid out clearly in your own private space, the whole way through.

01

We understand your business

It starts with a real conversation, not a sales pitch. We talk about your business, your customers, and what's actually getting in the way. Sometimes the answer is a new website. Sometimes it isn't — and I'll tell you either way. You'll leave with more clarity than you came in with, whether or not we work together.

You feel: heard, not sold to
02

We plan the website and SEO foundation

Before any design happens, we map out what your site needs to do and how people will actually find you. I plan the pages, the structure, and the SEO groundwork around your real goals — so the site we build is set up to bring in customers, not just look good.

You feel: confident there's a real strategy behind it
03

We build, with you in the loop

As your site comes together, you're never left guessing. You can see what's done, what's in progress, and what's coming next. When it's time for your input, you'll know exactly what to look at — and the work moves forward without a tangle of email threads.

You feel: looped in, never chasing
04

You're never left guessing

Every update, file, and approval lives in your private portal as you go — not because that's the pitch, but because that's how the project stays organized on my end too. See exactly what that looks like →

You feel: in control, never in the dark
05

We launch

Launch day isn't a scramble. By the time your site goes live, everything has already been reviewed, approved, and accounted for. What you owe and what you've paid has been clear the whole way — no surprise invoices, no awkward money conversations. You launch with confidence, knowing nothing was missed.

You feel: calm on a day that's usually stressful
