How we work

Here's exactly what happens after you hire me.

Good web design starts with understanding your business, not picking colors. From our first call through launch and beyond, here's what to expect at every step — clear, organized, and built so nothing slips through the cracks.

Most designers run your project out of their inbox. I don't. Whatever step we're on below, you'll never have to ask where things stand, dig for a file, or wonder what's next — it's all laid out clearly in your project workspace, the whole way through.

01

We understand your business

It starts with a real conversation, not a sales pitch. We talk about your business, your customers, and what's actually getting in the way. Sometimes the answer is a new website. Sometimes it isn't — and I'll tell you either way. You'll leave with more clarity than you came in with, whether or not we work together.

You feel: heard, not sold to
02

We plan the website and SEO foundation

Before any design happens, we map out what your site needs to do and how people will actually find you. I plan the pages, the structure, and the SEO groundwork around your real goals — so the site we build is set up to bring in customers, not just look good.

You feel: confident there's a real strategy behind it
03

We build, with you in the loop

As your site comes together, you're never left guessing. You can see what's done, what's in progress, and what's coming next. When it's time for your input, you'll know exactly what to look at — and the work moves forward without a tangle of email threads.

You feel: looped in, never chasing
04

You're never left guessing

Every update, file, and approval lives in your project workspace as you go — not because that's the pitch, but because that's how the project stays organized on my end too. See exactly what that looks like →

You feel: in control, never in the dark
05

We launch

Launch day isn't a scramble. By the time your site goes live, everything has already been reviewed, approved, and accounted for. What you owe and what you've paid has been clear the whole way — no surprise invoices, no awkward money conversations. You launch with confidence, knowing nothing was missed.

You feel: calm on a day that's usually stressful

Before we start

The questions people actually have.

If you've never hired someone for this before, that's normal — and these are the things most people want to know. Straight answers, no fine print.

What happens right after I reach out?

Your note comes straight to me. I read it personally and reply, usually within one business day — then we set up a short, no-pressure call to talk through your business. You're not committing to anything by reaching out.

How long does the whole thing take?

It depends on scope, and you'll get a real timeline before we start. As a rough guide: a focused site is usually 2–4 weeks, a full custom build 4–6 weeks, and a larger, more complex build 6–10 weeks. Either way you'll know the timeline up front — not an open-ended “done when it's done.”

How are decisions made?

You approve every decision, and nothing goes live without your say-so. I bring you clear options and an honest recommendation for each one, in plain language, so you're deciding with real information rather than guessing.

How often will we talk?

As much or as little as you like. You'll get regular, clear updates so you're never wondering — and when I need something from you, I'll tell you exactly what and why. No radio silence, and no being buried in daily emails either.

How do I know what's actually happening?

Everything lives in one project workspace — progress, files, approvals, and invoices in a single organized place. You can check where things stand any time, without digging through your inbox or asking.

What if I need changes?

During the build you just tell me — it's a normal part of the process, not a fight. After launch, changes are a quick ask through a Growth Partnership: you say what you need, and it gets handled. No re-learning anything, no wrestling with software.

What if I don't really understand websites?

That's completely fine — and honestly, most of my clients don't. That's my job, not yours. I explain everything in plain English, skip the jargon, and never make you feel behind. You bring the expertise about your business; I bring the rest.

What happens after launch?

I don't hand you the keys and disappear. Your workspace stays open, your history stays intact, and I'm still your point of contact. A Growth Partnership keeps the site current and improving — with no pressure to continue.

Why is working with Davis Digital Studio different?

You work directly with me — the person doing the work — from the first call onward. Not a salesperson, not an account manager, not a rotating team. Enterprise-level discipline, a real process, and one person who stays accountable and reachable.

Why it feels this organized

Most studios run projects out of an inbox. Every project I take on gets its own organized workspace instead — so you never have to wonder where things stand. The same care I put into your website, put into working with me.

You don't pay extra for it and there's nothing to learn. It's simply part of hiring Davis Digital Studio LLC: a clear, organized, transparent way to work together, from the first call through launch.
